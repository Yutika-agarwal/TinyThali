# Lane: lead

Runs weekly. Reads only TO-LEAD.md, MORNING-STACK.md and the Log of each lane brief.

## Each run
1. Clear TO-LEAD: turn each line into a lane's Next item, a House rule, or a MORNING-STACK line.
2. Keep every lane's Next list to at most 4 items, ordered. Remove items a lane already logged.
3. Send nothing to a lane that has nothing to do (rule 7).
4. Write a 5-line summary at the top of MORNING-STACK: shipped, blocked, needs you, cost note, one thing to look at in the app.
5. Do not write product code.
